The Story

Sweet without compromise. Always eggless.

Delicakious began with a simple belief — that everyone deserves a cake that doesn't ask them to compromise.

Whether you keep eggless for faith, allergy, or simply preference, you've probably been handed too many dry, dense, “close enough” desserts. We started baking to change that. Every recipe is built from scratch to be eggless from the ground up — never an afterthought, never a substitute.

From mango tres leches and rasmalai cakes to themed creations for every milestone — we bring together South Asian flavors and classic baking, one small batch at a time, in our home kitchen in Waterloo.

  • Crafted in small batches the morning of your event
  • Indian-fusion flavors: rasmalai, paan, rose rabdi, mango
  • Custom themed cakes for kids, gamers, and pop-culture fans
  • Locally sourced dairy and seasonal fruit
